Locations are major on-screen sites compiled from well-documented production history, not an exhaustive shot list. Coordinates are approximate (city or landmark level). Cádiz plays Havana in Die Another Day, and the Ouarzazate desert doubles for Afghanistan in The Living Daylights. The 1967 Casino Royale and 1983 Never Say Never Again were made outside Eon Productions and are filed as unsanctioned. The flat view uses the Equal Earth projection, which keeps every country at its true relative size.
